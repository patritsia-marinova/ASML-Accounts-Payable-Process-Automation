# 02 – Process Improvement & Requirements

## Overview

The second phase of the ASML project focused on designing an improved Accounts Payable process and defining the requirements needed to support process optimisation.

Using the bottlenecks identified during the business analysis, I developed a future-state process (SOLL) focused on digital invoice handling, automated validation, improved communication, and Straight-Through Processing (STP).

I also developed requirements, KPIs and KSFs, stakeholder analysis, and business recommendations to support the proposed improvements.

## Process Improvement Activities

### 1. [SOLL Process Model](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/ASML-Accounts-Payable-Process-Automation/blob/main/02%20%E2%80%93%20Process%20Improvement%20%26%20Requirements/SOLL%20ASML%20.pdf)

I developed a BPMN model representing the proposed future-state Accounts Payable process.

The improved workflow focused on receiving invoices digitally, automatically validating invoice information, and matching invoices with purchase orders and delivery information.

When the required information matches correctly, invoices can be processed automatically, reducing the need for manual intervention. I also incorporated exception handling and improved communication between departments and suppliers.

### 2. [Requirements Documentation](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/ASML-Accounts-Payable-Process-Automation/blob/main/02%20%E2%80%93%20Process%20Improvement%20%26%20Requirements/ASML%20Requirements.xlsb.xlsx)

I developed requirements based on the bottlenecks identified during the business analysis and the improvements proposed in the SOLL process.

The requirements focused on automated invoice validation, purchase order matching, invoice visibility, delivery confirmation, exception handling, and communication.

This helped translate the identified business problems into clearer expectations for the proposed technical solution.

### 3. [KPIs & KSFs](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/ASML-Accounts-Payable-Process-Automation/blob/main/02%20%E2%80%93%20Process%20Improvement%20%26%20Requirements/KPI's%20%26%20KSF's%20ASML.docx)

I defined Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and Key Success Factors (KSFs) to support evaluating the effectiveness of the proposed Accounts Payable process.

The KPIs focused on measuring process performance and efficiency, while the KSFs identified important conditions for successfully improving the workflow.

This connected the proposed process improvements with measurable business objectives and helped establish how the solution's performance could be evaluated.

### 4. [Stakeholder Analysis – Power vs Interest](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/ASML-Accounts-Payable-Process-Automation/blob/main/02%20%E2%80%93%20Process%20Improvement%20%26%20Requirements/P%20vs%20I%20Stakeholder%20Analysis.png)

I developed a Power vs Interest matrix to analyse the stakeholders involved in the Accounts Payable process.

The analysis considered stakeholder influence, interest, and involvement in the proposed improvements.

Following feedback during a checkpoint discussion, I reviewed the positioning of stakeholders to improve the accuracy of the analysis.

### 5. [Business Advice](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/ASML-Accounts-Payable-Process-Automation/blob/main/02%20%E2%80%93%20Process%20Improvement%20%26%20Requirements/Business%20Advice%20%E2%80%93%20ASML.docx)

I prepared a business advice document outlining recommendations for improving the Accounts Payable process.

The advice brought together the identified bottlenecks, proposed process improvements, automation opportunities, and organisational considerations.

This helped translate the findings from the business analysis and process improvement phases into recommendations for a more efficient and automated Accounts Payable workflow.

## Outcome

This phase resulted in an improved SOLL process supported by requirements documentation, KPI and KSF definitions, stakeholder analysis, and business recommendations.

These deliverables demonstrated how the inefficiencies identified in the existing process could be translated into practical improvements and measurable business objectives.

Together, they provided the foundation for developing the Power Automate prototype in the next phase.
