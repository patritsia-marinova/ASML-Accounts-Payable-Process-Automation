# ASML – Accounts Payable Process Automation

## Project Overview

During Semester 3 of my ICT & Business studies at Fontys University of Applied Sciences, I worked on an individual project focused on improving ASML's Accounts Payable (AP) process through business analysis, process optimisation, requirements engineering, and automation.

The existing process relied heavily on manual invoice handling, with approximately 90% of invoices received on paper. Missing purchase order (PO) numbers, manual validation, and communication between departments and suppliers created additional administrative work and delays.

The goal was to investigate these inefficiencies, design an improved future-state process, and develop an automation prototype demonstrating how digital invoice processing and Straight-Through Processing (STP) could reduce manual activities.

## From Business Analysis to Automation

The project followed three main stages:

1. **Business Analysis:** Investigated the existing Accounts Payable process through client interview notes, Fishbone Analysis, BPMN modelling, and bottleneck identification.

2. **Process Improvement & Requirements:** Designed an improved future-state process (SOLL), developed requirements and KPIs, analysed stakeholders, and prepared business recommendations to support process optimisation.

3. **Power Automate Development:** Developed and tested a Power Automate prototype that simulates automated invoice processing, validation, exception handling, and communication.

## Proposed Accounts Payable Solution

The proposed SOLL process focused on transforming the manual and paper-based workflow into a more digital and automated process.

The improvements included:

- Digital invoice receipt and validation.
- Automated matching of invoices with purchase orders and delivery information.
- Straight-Through Processing (STP) for correctly matched invoices.
- Exception handling for missing or incorrect information.
- Improved communication between departments and suppliers.
- Reduced manual intervention during invoice processing.

To demonstrate the technical feasibility of parts of this process, I developed a Power Automate prototype that evaluates predefined conditions, processes records, handles exceptions, and sends automated notifications.

The workflow was developed as a proof of concept rather than implemented as a production solution at ASML.

## Project Outcome

The project resulted in a proposed improvement of ASML's Accounts Payable process, supported by business analysis, process modelling, requirements documentation, and a tested Power Automate prototype.

The proposed process demonstrated how digital invoice handling, automated validation, and structured exception handling could reduce manual activities and improve process efficiency.

This project allowed me to combine **business process analysis, requirements engineering, and technical automation development**, strengthening my ability to translate business problems into practical IT solutions.
